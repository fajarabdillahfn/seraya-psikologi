# 75. Start Launch with One Confirmed Psychologist

## Status

Accepted for launch planning; profile content is recorded from the supplied business brief. Professional registration identifiers are intentionally redacted in shared artifacts and require verification before publication.

## Context

The launch catalog needs a psychologist assignment and public professional profile. Only one psychologist's data is currently available; the other planned profile slots have no confirmed data.

## Decision

Start launch planning with **Fuja Rahayu Kinanti, S.Psi., Psikolog** as the only confirmed launch psychologist.

Public/profile brief:

- role: Psikolog Umum;
- experience: 50+ handled sessions; 0–1 year experience range as supplied;
- approach: warm, empathic, client-centered support that gives clients space to understand themselves, explore concerns, and find more adaptive steps;
- areas of interest: self-development/self-understanding, emotion management, confidence/self-esteem, stress and everyday pressure, interpersonal relationships/communication, relationship concerns, adjustment to change and life stages, academic challenges, work/career challenges, decision-making, and potential development;
- education: S1 Psikologi, Universitas Gadjah Mada (2015); Program Pendidikan Profesi Psikolog, Universitas Muhammadiyah Malang (2026);
- services mentioned in the supplied profile: psychological counseling and psychological assessment, although assessment remains deferred from the launch catalog by ADR 0071.

STR/SILP values are not copied into shared PRD/domain artifacts; they are represented as `[REDACTED — verify before publication]`. No public credential claim is considered approved until Admin verifies the source documents and publication consent.

Fuja is confirmed to deliver the launch individual counseling variants and the couple counseling package. Concrete recurring availability, mode/location schedule, and any case-specific clinical suitability boundary remain open. A placeholder `anytime/anyplace` schedule is acceptable for PRD/design and is not a PRD closure blocker; it must be replaced before production slots are published.

## Consequences

Positive:

- launch catalog can begin with one known professional profile;
- no invented data is created for the other four profile slots;
- credential verification and publication consent become explicit gates.

Costs and constraints:

- capacity is limited to one psychologist at launch;
- all published slots need a confirmed recurring schedule and mode/location rules;
- couple counseling assignment needs explicit confirmation;
- adding another psychologist requires verified profile/credential data and an audited staff/catalog setup.

## Open follow-up

Confirm Fuja's production recurring availability, online/offline eligibility, offline venue, booking horizon, profile photo/publication consent, and credential verification. The design placeholder `anytime/anyplace` is intentionally non-blocking for PRD closure; keep other psychologist profiles unpublished until their data is confirmed.
