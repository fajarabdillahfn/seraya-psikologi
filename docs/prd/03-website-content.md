# PRD 03 — Website Content

## Goal

Give visitors enough trustworthy information to understand Seraya, decide whether SERAYA PULANG fits, and start booking without overpromising.

## Public pages

- Home
- SERAYA PULANG
- Fuja profile
- FAQ
- Safety/crisis boundary
- Privacy
- Informed consent
- Cancellation/refund policy
- Booking: offering → slot → intake → payment handoff

## Content rules

- Public brand name: **Seraya Psikologi**.
- Do not present Seraya as emergency care.
- Do not promise diagnosis, instant cure, guaranteed outcomes, or forced recovery.
- Only published and verified professional information may be shown.
- SERAYA BERDAYA, SERAYA BERSAMA, and SERAYA BERBAGI are content-only until explicitly made bookable.
- Cancellation/refund public copy points to Admin WhatsApp; no public cancellation/refund form.

## Copy still needing review

- Consent wording.
- Privacy notice and retention wording.
- Crisis/referral wording.
- Fuja credentials/profile evidence.
- Real online/offline service instructions.
- Payment instructions and Admin contact.

## Acceptance checks

- Every CTA points to an existing route.
- No placeholder credential, bank account, venue, or phone number is presented as real.
- Mobile layout is readable and forms expose validation errors.
- Accessibility/performance/SEO checks are recorded before launch.
